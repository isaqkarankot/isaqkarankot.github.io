Mahmad Isaq Karankot

Ph.D. Candidate, Electrical & Computer Engineering

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the BMW Lab at Montana State University, where I develop artificial intelligence methods for Earth observation and environmental intelligence.

My research focuses on multimodal learning by combining hyperspectral imagery, LiDAR, thermal imagery, and satellite data to build accurate, robust, and explainable AI systems. I am interested in representation learning, computer vision, deep learning, foundation models, and trustworthy AI for remote sensing.

My current work explores how multimodal sensing can improve vegetation characterization, wildfire and prescribed fire assessment, ecosystem monitoring, and geospatial decision-making.

Before starting my Ph.D., I worked as a Software Engineer for more than six years at Accenture, Brillio, and Born Group, developing enterprise-scale software systems. This experience helps me build AI solutions that are both research-driven and practical for real-world applications.

I am passionate about developing AI that bridges machine learning research with real-world environmental challenges.
